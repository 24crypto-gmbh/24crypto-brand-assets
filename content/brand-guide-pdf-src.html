  • Clear space around the logo ≈ its own height. Never stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects. Min width on screen: logo 110 px, mark 24 px.
  • On busy footage, place the white-text logo over a darkened area or navy overlay — never directly on bright detail.

Typography — Inter

Headlines: Inter Bold/ExtraBold.
Key words in lime.

Overlines: caps + letterspacing

Body & subtitles: Inter Regular/Medium. Zinc #A1A1AA on dark, #52525B on light. Fallback font: Helvetica Neue / Arial.

Video rules

  • Look: ~70% navy, 20% white/zinc text, 10% lime — same feel as 24crypto.ch.
  • Watermark: white "24" mark, top-right, ~40% opacity, all videos.
  • Subtitles: Inter Bold, white, key words in lime. Always burn in.
  • End card: navy background, logo + lime 24crypto.ch (template: instagram-story).
  • Tone: calm, precise, Swiss. No hype, no memes, no FOMO countdowns.
Compliance — every published video/post: no price predictions or promised returns; market content carries "Not investment advice. Crypto assets are volatile and may lose value." Regulatory wording is always exactly: "Member of VQF, a FINMA-recognised self-regulatory organisation (SRO)" — never "FINMA licensed". Don't use the Swiss federal cross in graphics (🇨🇭 emoji in captions is fine).  ·  Assets: see the 24crypto brand kit (logos/, social/, content/).
