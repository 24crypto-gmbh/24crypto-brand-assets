# 24crypto — Brand Guidelines (v1, July 2026)

Quick-reference guide for anyone producing 24crypto marketing material, social posts or videos.

## 1. Logo system

| Asset | File | Use on |
|---|---|---|
| Primary logo (dark text) | `logos/svg/24crypto-logo-color-dark-text.svg` | White / light backgrounds |
| Primary logo (white text) | `logos/svg/24crypto-logo-color-white-text.svg` | Navy / dark backgrounds, photos |
| Mono black | `logos/svg/24crypto-logo-mono-black.svg` | Print, invoices, single-colour contexts |
| Mono white | `logos/svg/24crypto-logo-mono-white.svg` | Dark single-colour contexts, video watermarks |
| "24" mark (lime) | `logos/svg/24crypto-mark-lime.svg` | Avatars, favicons, watermarks, merch |
| Square avatar navy | `logos/svg/24crypto-avatar-navy.svg` | Social profile pictures (primary) |
| Square avatar lime | `logos/svg/24crypto-avatar-lime.svg` | Alternative profile picture, stickers |
| Square avatar white | `logos/svg/24crypto-avatar-white.svg` | Light-theme app contexts |

PNG exports of every variant are in `logos/png/` (500–4000 px wide; marks 256–2048 px).

### Rules
- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the "2" glyph (roughly the logo height). Never crowd it with text or other logos.
- **Minimum size:** full logo ≥ 110 px wide on screen; mark ≥ 24 px.
- **Do not** stretch, rotate, recolour, add shadows/gradients/outlines, or place the lime mark on a lime background.
- On busy photos, put the white-text logo inside a navy overlay or darkened area.
- The navy avatar is the default profile picture on every platform — consistent avatar = recognisable brand.

## 2. Colours

| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 Lime | `#d0dd27` | 208 221 39 | Brand accent, CTAs, the "24" mark |
| Deep Navy | `#020816` | 2 8 22 | Primary dark background |
| Ink | `#18181b` | 24 24 27 | Body text on light backgrounds |
| White | `#ffffff` | 255 255 255 | Text on dark, light backgrounds |
| Zinc | `#a1a1aa` | 161 161 170 | Secondary text, captions |
| Lime dark | `#a3ad13` | 163 173 19 | Hover states, lime on light bg when contrast needed |

Ratio guideline for social visuals: ~70% navy, ~20% white/zinc text, ~10% lime. Lime is an accent — never a full background for text-heavy layouts (exception: the lime avatar and small CTA pills with navy text).

## 3. Typography

- **Brand font: Inter** (free, Google Fonts / fontsource — same as 24crypto.ch).
- Headlines: Inter Bold / ExtraBold, tight line-height (1.05–1.15). Sentence case ("Buy Bitcoin in minutes."), full stop for punch.
- Labels / overlines: Inter SemiBold, ALL CAPS, generous letter-spacing (e.g. "REGULATED SWISS CRYPTO EXCHANGE").
- Body: Inter Regular, `#a1a1aa` on dark, `#52525b` on light.
- Fallback when Inter is unavailable (e.g. some video tools): Helvetica Neue or Arial.

## 4. Voice & tone

- **Positioning:** the regulated Swiss way to buy and sell crypto — transparent fees, fast onboarding, Swiss-grade compliance. Member of VQF (SRO) under Swiss AML regulations.
- **Personality:** precise, calm, trustworthy — Swiss banking confidence with a modern, direct edge. Never hype, never "moon/lambo" memes, no FOMO pressure.
- Speak plainly: "Buy Bitcoin in minutes. Fully compliant." beats "Revolutionary next-gen trading solution".
- Languages: English + German (site is EN/DE — mirror important posts in both).

## 5. Compliance guardrails (every post)

- No price predictions, no promised or implied returns, no "guaranteed", no investment advice. Educational framing only.
- When talking about markets add: "Not investment advice. Crypto assets are volatile and may lose value."
- Always accurate about status: "Member of VQF, a FINMA-recognised self-regulatory organisation (SRO)" — do **not** claim "FINMA licensed/regulated bank".
- Don't use the Swiss federal cross in marketing (restricted by Swiss law) — say "Swiss" in words, use the flag emoji 🇨🇭 in captions only.
